## Short post

You can't smuggle a chip through a fibre-optic cable. But you can rent one.

That loophole just closed. A new US rule says data centers anywhere in the world need a licence to rent serious AI computing power to Chinese companies, and shipping top AI chips to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil now needs one too.

Quick test for your Tuesday: do you know which country your AI actually runs in?
